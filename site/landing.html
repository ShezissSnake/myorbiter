orbiter is a desktop companion for Warframe, for Linux, the Steam Deck, Windows and macOS. It reads what TennoScope stores on your PC (the game's log, the fissure reward screen, and your inventory with Full mode on) and answers what the game doesn't: which reward to take, what you can sell and for how much, what's left to master and how hard it is to get, which relics finish your sets.

It also keeps your warframe.market listings at the cheapest price in game, never under the floors you set nor under what a prime part's ducats are worth to you, marks sales sold and gives +rep after a trade. It runs on your computer.

Free software · GPL-3.0-only · for Linux, the Steam Deck, Windows and macOS

Full mode is your call

{{fullModeWarning}}

It's off unless you turn it on. Without it, TennoScope reads only the game's log and the reward screen, and orbiter shows everything that doesn't need your inventory.

It acts on your warframe.market account

Once you sign in, what you press and the automatic features you turn on (repricing, marking sales sold, +rep, hiding your last copies, holding your online status) list, change and close orders and post reviews as you.

Download

{{downloads}}

One download, nothing else to install: the Linux and Windows ones carry TennoScope inside them. On Xbox, PlayStation, Switch or mobile, use the same download on a PC or Mac and pick your platform in Settings: the market, fissures, the world's clocks and the riven finder work; the collection pages need the game on the PC.
