Before you start

Full mode is your call

{{fullModeWarning}}

It's off unless you turn it on. Without it, TennoScope reads only the game's log and the reward screen, and MyOrbiter shows everything that doesn't need your inventory.

It acts on your warframe.market account

Once you sign in, what you press and the automatic features you turn on (repricing, marking sales sold, +rep, hiding your last copies, holding your online status) list, change and close orders and post reviews as you.

Download

{{downloads}}

TennoScope is inside: nothing else to install. On Xbox, PlayStation, Switch or mobile, run it on a PC and pick your platform in Settings; the collection pages need the game on the PC.
